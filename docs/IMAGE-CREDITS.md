## Generated images

The twelve `home/concern-*.jpg` files behind "Real results in eight weeks" and "Made for what your skin needs" are **not photographs**. They were generated with OpenAI `gpt-image-2` at 1024x768 (4:3, the aspect the tile renders). Nobody in them is a real person, so there is no model release and no other site is using the same face.

The six week-0 frames were generated as one series: every prompt carried the same house style — medium telephoto at f/2, soft warm window daylight from the left, plain oat plaster wall, no makeup, no jewellery, no hands in frame, skin left unretouched — and varied only the woman and the concern she shows. That shared spec is what makes them read as one sitting rather than six stock photos.

The six `-after.jpg` frames are edits of their own before frame, passed back in as the reference so the model, pose, crop, light and wall stay put and only the skin changes. Prompt them as "a second photograph from the same sitting", not as a change to a person: the edit endpoint refuses most prompts phrased as altering someone's face, and four of six failed until they were rewritten that way.

Two things to weigh before these ship as anything but placeholders. A before-and-after is a claim, and these afters were invented rather than observed — the Pair design carries the study footnote for that reason, and the dark-spots after fades further than eight weeks of anything honestly would. They are also generated, not photographs. Replace them with the production shoot before launch and keep the file names.
