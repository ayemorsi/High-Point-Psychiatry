This policy covers the website at {store.domain}, run by {store.legalName} ("{store.name}", "we", "us"), a psychiatric practice in {store.region}.

It describes information collected through this website only. How we use and protect your health information as a patient is described in our Notice of Privacy Practices, which we provide when you become a patient.

## What we collect

- **When you send a message.** Your name, email address, phone number if you give it, and what you write. Please don't include detailed medical information in the website form.
- **When you browse.** Basic technical information such as your device, browser, approximate location from your IP address and the pages you visit, used to keep the site working.

## How we use it

- To reply to your message and, if you ask, to schedule an appointment
- To keep the website secure and working properly

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Who we share it with

Only the service providers that host this website and deliver our email, and only as needed to provide those services. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

We keep website messages only as long as needed to respond and for our normal records. If you become a patient, your records are kept as required by law.

## Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold from your website messages, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing [{store.email.display}]({store.email.href}).

## Emergencies

This website and its contact form are not monitored for emergencies. If you are in crisis, call or text {store.crisis.line} or call 911.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
