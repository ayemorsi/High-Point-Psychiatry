Psychiatric medication management at High Point Psychiatry goes far beyond simply writing a prescription. Ahmed Morsi, PMHNP-BC conducts thorough evaluations to understand your full clinical picture before recommending any medication — taking into account your history, lifestyle, current symptoms, and personal goals.

## What to Expect

Your first appointment includes a comprehensive psychiatric evaluation. From there, Ahmed will discuss all appropriate treatment options with you, including the potential benefits and side effects of any medications being considered. You will always be an active participant in these decisions.

Follow-up appointments monitor your progress, adjust dosages as needed, and ensure your treatment continues to align with your evolving needs.

## Conditions We Treat with Medication Management

- Major Depressive Disorder
- Generalized Anxiety Disorder
- Bipolar Disorder
- ADHD (Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder)
- OCD (Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder)
- PTSD and Trauma-Related Disorders
- Schizophrenia and Psychotic Disorders
- Panic Disorder
- Personality Disorders

## In-Person & Telehealth Available

Medication management appointments are available in-person at our Flint, MI office or via secure telehealth video for patients across Michigan.
