Mood disorders and trauma-related conditions can profoundly affect every area of a person's life — relationships, work, physical health, and sense of self. High Point Psychiatry offers specialized care for these complex conditions, delivered with the sensitivity and expertise they require.

## Mood Disorders We Treat

- Bipolar I and Bipolar II Disorder
- Cyclothymic Disorder
- Schizoaffective Disorder
- Major Depressive Disorder with Psychotic Features
- Borderline Personality Disorder (mood component)

## Trauma & Stress-Related Conditions

- Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)
- Acute Stress Disorder
- Adjustment Disorders
- Complex Trauma (C-PTSD)
- Trauma from adverse childhood experiences (ACEs)

## OCD and Related Disorders

- Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD)
- Body Dysmorphic Disorder
- Hoarding Disorder
- Trichotillomania and related conditions

## Our Trauma-Informed Approach

All care at High Point Psychiatry is delivered through a trauma-informed lens. This means we:

- Prioritize your sense of safety throughout every interaction
- Recognize that past experiences shape present symptoms
- Never push you faster than you are ready to go
- Work collaboratively with any therapists or counselors already part of your care team

Healing is possible. You deserve care that meets you where you are.
