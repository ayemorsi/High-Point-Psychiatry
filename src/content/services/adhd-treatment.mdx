Adult ADHD is frequently underdiagnosed and misunderstood. Many adults with ADHD spent years being told they were lazy, disorganized, or not living up to their potential — not realizing that there was a neurological explanation for their challenges. High Point Psychiatry provides thorough ADHD evaluations and evidence-based treatment for adults across Michigan.

## Signs of Adult ADHD

- Difficulty sustaining attention on tasks or conversations
- Chronic disorganization or losing important items
- Impulsivity in decisions or interrupting others
- Difficulty following through on tasks or projects
- Emotional dysregulation or low frustration tolerance
- Procrastination and time management challenges
- Hyperactivity or a sense of internal restlessness

## Evaluation Process

A comprehensive ADHD evaluation at High Point Psychiatry includes a thorough review of your history, current symptoms, and their impact on your daily functioning. Ahmed may use validated rating scales and screening tools to support the diagnostic process.

Because other conditions (anxiety, depression, sleep disorders, thyroid issues) can mimic ADHD, a careful differential diagnosis is essential.

## Treatment Options

Treatment is always individualized and may include:

- **Stimulant medications** (when clinically appropriate) — the most studied and effective class of ADHD medications
- **Non-stimulant alternatives** — for patients for whom stimulants are contraindicated
- **Behavioral strategies** — practical systems for organization, time management, and focus
- **Comorbidity treatment** — addressing co-occurring anxiety, depression, or mood issues alongside ADHD

Getting the right diagnosis and the right treatment can be genuinely life-changing. We are here to help you take that first step.
