A comprehensive psychiatric evaluation is the critical first step in building an effective treatment plan. At High Point Psychiatry, evaluations are thorough, unhurried, and deeply person-centered. Ahmed Morsi, PMHNP-BC takes the time to understand not just your symptoms, but your history, your relationships, and your goals.

## What the Evaluation Includes

Your initial evaluation typically lasts 60–90 minutes and covers:

- **Presenting symptoms** — What you're experiencing and how long it's been happening
- **Psychiatric history** — Previous diagnoses, treatments, and medications
- **Medical history** — Physical health conditions and medications that may intersect with mental health
- **Family history** — Genetic and environmental factors
- **Social history** — Life circumstances, relationships, work, and community
- **Mental status examination** — A structured clinical assessment of your current cognitive and emotional state

## After the Evaluation

At the conclusion of your evaluation, Ahmed will share his clinical impressions with you, discuss potential diagnoses, and collaboratively build a treatment plan. This may include medication, therapy recommendations, lifestyle guidance, or referrals to additional specialists as needed.

## Conditions Commonly Diagnosed

- Depression and Mood Disorders
- Anxiety Disorders
- ADHD
- OCD
- PTSD and Trauma Disorders
- Bipolar Disorder
- Psychotic Disorders
- Personality Disorders
