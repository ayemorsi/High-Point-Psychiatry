High Point Psychiatry offers telehealth psychiatric services to patients throughout Michigan. Whether you live in a rural area with limited local mental health providers, have a busy schedule, or simply prefer the privacy and comfort of your own space, telehealth makes it easier to get the care you need.

## How Telehealth Works

Telehealth appointments at High Point Psychiatry are conducted via a secure, HIPAA-compliant video platform. You will receive a link before your appointment — all you need is a smartphone, tablet, or computer with a camera and a private space.

Telehealth visits are clinically equivalent to in-person appointments for most services, including:

- Initial psychiatric evaluations
- Medication management and follow-ups
- Ongoing treatment and monitoring

## Who is Telehealth Right For?

Telehealth is a great fit if you:

- Live outside Genesee County or the greater Flint area
- Have transportation limitations
- Prefer appointments from home
- Have a schedule that makes in-person visits difficult
- Are a current patient traveling within Michigan

## Available Statewide

High Point Psychiatry is licensed to provide telehealth services to patients across the state of Michigan — from the Upper Peninsula to metro Detroit and everywhere in between.
